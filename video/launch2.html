What should I check before flashing the board?

Before you flash it, confirm the board runs at 3.3 V logic and that the USB cable carries data, not just power. Most failed uploads come from the cable. Then hold the BOOT button while the upload starts if the port is detected but the sketch will not transfer. If it still fails, check the datasheet for the strapping pins before blaming the code.
