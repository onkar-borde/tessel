    Settings & data

    Behaviour

    Colour names

    Give the four colours a meaning (for example Claim, Stat, Term, To check). Names appear in tooltips, filters, Markdown and shared pages.

    Need more than four? Add up to four custom colours; they get quick keys 5–8.

    Your data

    Highlights are stored in this browser, on this device, and are never sent anywhere. They survive restarts and updates, and clearing your history or cookies does not remove them. Uninstalling the extension or removing the browser profile does, so keep a backup file: it lives outside the browser, in your Downloads folder.

    Keyboard

    1 2 3 4Highlight with colour 1 to 4 (text selected); 5–8 are your custom colours
    HHighlight in the last colour you used
    CHighlight and add a comment
    Alt+↓ / ↑Next / previous highlight, then Enter to open it
    Alt+Shift+LShow / hide the highlights panel on the page
    Ctrl/⌘+EnterFinish a comment
    EscClose the toolbar, comment box or panel
    Alt+Shift+H / CSame as H / C; remap on your browser's extension-shortcuts page

    On Mac, Alt is Option. Single keys never act while you are typing in a text box.
