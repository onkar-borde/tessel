# Privacy policy

This extension collects nothing. It has no servers, accounts, analytics or telemetry, and makes no network requests.

- **What it stores:** the passages you highlight, your comments, tags and settings, in the browser's local extension storage on your device.
- **Where it runs:** only on the chat sites it lists (claude.ai, chatgpt.com). On those pages it reads the text you select, to anchor your highlight.
- **Sharing:** nothing is sent anywhere. A shared chat is a file you create and send yourself.
- **Backups (optional):** if you turn on automatic backups, the extension saves JSON files to a "Tessel backups" folder inside your Downloads folder. They stay on your device.
- **Removal:** uninstalling the extension deletes its stored data. Backup files in Downloads are yours to keep or delete.

Independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic or OpenAI.
