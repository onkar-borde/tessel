      Settings

      Keyboard

      1 2 3 4Highlight with colour 1 to 4
      HHighlight in the last colour you used
      CHighlight and add a comment
      Alt+↓ / ↑Next / previous highlight
      EnterOpen the highlight you just jumped to
      Alt+Shift+LShow / hide the highlights panel
      Ctrl/⌘+EnterFinish a comment
      EscClose the toolbar, comment box or panel
      Alt+Shift+H / CSame as H / C; remap on your browser's extension-shortcuts page

      Keys 1–4, H and C only act while text in the conversation is selected, and never while you are typing in a text box. On Mac, Alt is Option.
